Castle artwork

“Landscape castle” by Kathrin Polikeit (Kitty), from Battle for Wesnoth. Copyright and artist are embedded in the image’s EXIF metadata. Licensed under the GNU GPL version 2 or later.

The original painting is distributed unmodified. Aetheria displays it with CSS cropping and a purple lighting overlay. It depicts fortified medieval architecture; it is not presented as a photograph of a named historical castle.
