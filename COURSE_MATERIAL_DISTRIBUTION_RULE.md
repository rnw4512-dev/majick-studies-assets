# Mandatory Course Material Distribution and OA Reasoning Rule

**HARD RULE — all future app and course projects.**

Any instructional material submitted through chat, imported documents, or an in-app upload must be processed once through the canonical course-specific coverage tracker and distributed to **every relevant surface**, never only one screen.

## Required pipeline

1. Identify the course, section, lesson, specific concepts, examples, misconceptions and unique question types. Never mix academic banks from different courses.
2. Compare against existing coverage; preserve new ideas and examples, avoid duplicate definitions and question IDs. Do not silently overwrite content.
3. Register material in the course's authoritative content store. Reuse this material through adapters rather than separate untracked copies.
4. Route to all applicable locations: section map, Learn Lab, lesson concept pages, Study Now, Grimoire/study guide, visual anchor charts, guided worked examples, independent practice, lesson and section quizzes, Game Realm, OA-style review, error/misconception remediation, mastery and progress reporting.
5. **Every suitable assessment concept must have OA-style applied and multi-step questions, step-by-step rationales, explicit wrong-answer traps, calculator/whiteboard setup when appropriate, and visual support aligned with the mathematics.** Easy vocabulary recall alone is insufficient.
6. Keep guided practice separate from independent mastery; assistance must not count as independent mastery.
7. Tag items with course, section, lesson, competency, skill, rigor, source/provenance and stable ID. Clearly label original simulated practice as non-official WGU questions.
8. Validate coverage: report per-surface inclusion, question counts by section, no duplicates, links and supported visuals.
9. Deliver the standard change-and-recovery report: files, SHA, actual build/deploy/browser test status, remaining issues, fallback and rollback without losing saved progress.

**Exception:** Some material does not belong in every view (e.g., private notes may not be appropriate for quizzes). Document why any destination was intentionally skipped. Never mislabel a page as updated if its adapter has not been connected.

For D772, Sections 1–3 must remain present in mixed reviews. Section-specific lesson modes must continue to respect their own boundaries.
